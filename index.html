Create your awards in three steps

1

Add nominations and nominees

Name your awards and add up to 5 nominations. For each one, search the Streams Charts database for a channel or type a nominee in by hand, whether that’s a mod, a clip title or a running joke.

2

Publish and share the link

Publishing gives your awards its own page. Copy the link into chat or post it to X - the preview card comes with it.

3

Close voting and announce the winners

Voting ends on the date you set or earlier if you close it yourself, and once you’ve announced the winners on stream, you publish the results so the page matches what chat just heard.
