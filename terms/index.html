Terms

Awards Maker is part of Streams Charts, operated by STREAMS CHARTS Public Organization. The Streams Charts terms of use apply here in full; these terms cover what is specific to running an awards show and voting in one.

What this service is

Awards Maker lets a streamer build an awards show, publish it as a public page, and let viewers vote on it. We host the page, count the votes and keep the results online afterwards. We do not organise the show, choose the categories or pick the nominees - the host does.

Your show is yours

The name, description, categories and nominees you enter stay your content. By publishing you give Streams Charts a non-exclusive licence to host, display, index and share that content for as long as the page exists, including in the public catalog and in previews when the link is shared.

What may go on a ballot

A nominee can be a channel we track, plain text, or - on the paid tier - an image or a clip. What it cannot be:

  • harassment of a person or a channel, including a category written to humiliate somebody;
  • hate speech, threats, or content sexualising a minor;
  • impersonation of another person, channel, brand or awards show;
  • content you have no right to publish, including someone else's clip or image passed off as material you may use;
  • anything unlawful under Ukrainian law or the law where you or your viewers are.

Anyone can flag a page with the Report button on it. We can unlist a page from the catalog, unpublish it, or remove it entirely when it breaks these rules - and we may do it without notice when the content is clearly illegal. Write to team@streamscharts.com to appeal a decision.

How voting works

  • One Twitch account casts one ballot per show, and one nominee per category.
  • A ballot cannot be changed after it is submitted.
  • Counts stay hidden from everyone but the host until the host publishes the winners.
  • On the free plan a show runs to 5 categories and 200 voters. Voting stops there; the votes already cast are kept.
  • We may discard votes that come from automated traffic or from accounts created to stuff a ballot, and we may stop a vote that is clearly being manipulated.

The host decides when the winners are announced. Where the result is a tie, the host picks - the product does not break ties for them.

Appearing in search and in the catalog

Every published show is listed in the public catalog; there is no way to publish a hidden one today. A page is opened to search engines once it has at least 3 categories, 2 nominees in each and a description. Below that it stays reachable by link but is not indexed.

Paying

The paid tier is a one-off purchase for one show - not a subscription - and it is not on sale yet. When it ships, payment, chargebacks and refunds follow the Streams Charts terms of use. Everything built on the free plan carries over. For a show we run with you, write to sales@streamscharts.com.

What we do not promise

The service is provided as is. We do not guarantee that a page is reachable without interruption, that every vote comes from a real person despite the checks above, or that the results of a public vote say anything beyond what people clicked. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses arising from a show you ran.

Age

In line with the Streams Charts terms, the service is not intended for users under 18. The privacy notice explains what happens if a child's data reaches us.

Changes and governing law

We can change these terms; the date at the top says when they last moved, and continuing to use the service after a change means accepting it. Ukrainian law governs, as in the main terms. Data handling is described in the Awards Maker privacy notice and the Streams Charts privacy policy.
