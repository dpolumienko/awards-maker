Picking the ones that work

Five is a good target, and it is what the free plan covers. A ballot people finish beats a longer one they abandon halfway, and your dashboard shows exactly where they stop: it counts how many ballots reached each category.

It has to have obvious candidates. "Best Moment" makes everyone think for a minute and then close the tab; "Rage Quit of the Year" already has three names attached to it in your chat. Write the category the way your community would say it.

Mixed in. One funny category after every two serious ones keeps a ballot moving, and the funny ones are what gets screenshotted when the winners are out.

Yes. Nominees do not have to be channels - type in any name, or use a clip or an image on the paid tier. The chat group works for a server as well as for a stream.
